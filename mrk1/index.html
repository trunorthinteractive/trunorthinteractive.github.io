About

MRK1 is an abandoned game simulator that finally has someone to play. Bend the rules, stack multipliers into huge combos, and pick your trade-offs in every shop. The further you go, the more it wants to know about the protocols it was trained to run.

  • Draft multipliers and slot custom ROM chips.
  • Chain scoring lines into combos to beat the target before turns run out.
  • Outscore a rogue simulator that mutates the rules and degrades further with each run.
