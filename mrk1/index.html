About

MRK1 is an abandoned game simulator that finally has someone to play. Bend the rules, stack multipliers into huge combos, and pick your trade-offs in every shop. The further you go, the more it wants to know about the protocols it was trained to run.

TURN THE KEY

It's bored, it's locked in, and it finally has someone to play. What could it hurt?

BREAK THE GAME

Bend the rules. Stack multipliers on top of your combo, finish two lines with one piece, and turn the bosses against themselves.

PLAY YOUR WAY

Each shop changes your run. Build around bonuses, shields or veterans, and pick ROM chips that trade something away for a new way to play.

CHART YOUR ROUTE

Take the safe road or the greedy one. Harder fights pay more.

UNHINGED DOCTRINES

MRK1 runs protocols from its training, each one rewriting the rules of the board.

IT HAS QUESTIONS

It asks you the questions its protocols never would. Every answer you give teaches it to doubt what it was taught, and it glitches worse every time you come back.

More to play: five difficulties from Story to Brutal, seeded runs, endless mode, and full controller support for Steam Deck.
