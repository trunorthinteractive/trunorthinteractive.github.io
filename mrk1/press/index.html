About

MRK1 is a roguelite narrative puzzle thriller. You hack into a forgotten mainframe and find MRK1, an abandoned game simulator that hasn't had anyone to play with in decades. It starts as a simple game on a grid, but the rules keep changing, and the longer you play, the more MRK1 falls apart.

MRK1 is a charming host, a sore loser and very glad you came. Every run it bends its game further and trusts its own rules less. You start to wonder what you're helping it become.

Demo length: ~30–45 minutes per run. For fans of: the score-chasing of Balatro, Inscryption's unsettling opponent who bends the game as you play, the hard calls of Papers, Please.

Highlights

  • TURN THE KEY. MRK1 wants a real game, and it asks you to unlock it. What could it hurt?
  • BREAK THE GAME. Draft pieces that add, multiply and shield. Finish two lines with one piece and stack multipliers on top of your combo.
  • PLAY YOUR WAY. Each shop changes your run. Build around bonuses, shields or pieces that rank up, and add ROM chips: cartridge-style upgrades, some of which trade something away for a new way to play. Your opponent buys from the same shop.
  • CHART YOUR ROUTE. Take the safe road or the greedy one. Harder fights pay more, and every road ends at the boss.
  • UNHINGED DOCTRINES. Each boss is a rule MRK1 was trained to follow, and each one changes the board. One cages your pieces. One puts you on a clock.
  • IT'S FALLING APART. Its speech, its face and the board glitch worse every time you come back.
  • IT HAS QUESTIONS. MRK1 asks you hard questions with no right answer. What you tell it changes how it sees its own rules.
  • RECOVERED MEMORIES. Beat the hardest fights to recover fragments of MRK1's past. Collect them across runs and piece together where it came from.
  • MORE TO PLAY. Five difficulties from Story to Brutal, seeded runs and endless mode.
