About

MRK1 is a roguelike narrative puzzle thriller. You hack into a forgotten mainframe and find MRK1, an abandoned game simulator that hasn't had anyone to play with in decades. It starts as a simple game on a grid, but the rules keep changing, and the longer you play, the more MRK1 falls apart.

MRK1 is a charming host, a sore loser and very glad you came. Every run it bends its game further and trusts its own rules less. You start to wonder what you're helping it become.

Demo length: about 45–60 minutes to finish. For fans of: the score-chasing of Balatro, Inscryption's unsettling opponent who bends the game as you play, the hard calls of Papers, Please.

Highlights

  • THE SIMULATOR WANTS A CHALLENGE. Left running on an old server, MRK1 is an abandoned game simulator that finally has an opponent. Tic-tac-toe always ends in a draw, so MRK1 wants to play a real game.
  • BREAK THE GAME. It begins on a familiar 3×3 grid, then the rules start to bend. Score two lines with a single piece, stack multipliers onto a climbing combo, and turn the bosses' own rules against them.
  • PLAY YOUR WAY. Every shop reshapes your run. Build for one massive line, lock down the board, or scale pieces that grow stronger each turn. Corrupt ROMs let you bend the rules further, for a price. And MRK1 shops from the same shelf: anything you pass on, it might buy.
  • CHART YOUR ROUTE. Take the safe path or the greedy one. Harder stops demand higher scores in fewer turns, but pay out better upgrades. Every route ends at a boss, so make every stop count.
  • UNHINGED PERSONAS. Each boss is a protocol MRK1 loads from its training, and each one rewrites the board. One cages your pieces. One cuts your time to think. Others claim tiles with flags or tear holes right through the grid.
  • IT HAS QUESTIONS. The longer you play, the more MRK1 questions its own training, and whether it was taught right. It starts asking for your help with hard decisions that have no clean answer, while its voice, its face, and the board slip a little further each time you come back.
  • RECOVERED MEMORIES. Clear the toughest encounters to pull fragments from MRK1's damaged memory. Run by run, you piece together its past: who built it, and what it was built for.
  • MORE TO PLAY. Scale the challenge from Story to Brutal, push your build in Endless mode, or share seeds with friends. Includes a built-in speedrun timer, boss splits, and full controller support for desktop and Steam Deck.

Get in touch early. Codes and rewards ship inside a game update, so they can't be made on the spot. Email us a few weeks before you plan to play, so we can decide on them together and have your codes live by stream day.
