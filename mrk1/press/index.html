About

MRK1 is a roguelike narrative puzzle thriller. You hack into a forgotten mainframe and find MRK1, an abandoned game simulator that hasn't had anyone to play with in decades. It starts as a simple game on a grid, but the rules keep changing, and the longer you play, the more MRK1 falls apart.

MRK1 is a charming host, a sore loser and very glad you came. Every run it bends its game further and trusts its own rules less. You start to wonder what you're helping it become.

Demo length: about 45–60 minutes to finish. For fans of: the score-chasing of Balatro, Inscryption's unsettling opponent who bends the game as you play, the hard calls of Papers, Please.

Highlights

  • TURN THE KEY. MRK1 wants a real game, and it asks you to unlock it. What could it hurt?
  • BREAK THE GAME. Draft pieces that add, multiply and shield. Finish two lines with one piece and stack multipliers on top of your combo.
  • PLAY YOUR WAY. Every shop changes your run. Build around one huge line, around blocking, or around pieces that grow with you. Some upgrades cost you something to play a different way. Your opponent buys from the same shop.
  • CHART YOUR ROUTE. Take the safe road or the greedy one. Harder fights pay more, and every road ends at the boss.
  • UNHINGED PERSONAS. Each boss is a protocol MRK1 loads from its training, and each one rewrites the rules of the board. One cages your pieces. One puts you on a clock.
  • IT'S FALLING APART. Its speech, its face and the board glitch worse every time you come back.
  • IT HAS QUESTIONS. MRK1 asks you hard questions with no right answer. What you tell it changes how it sees its own rules.
  • RECOVERED MEMORIES. Beat the hardest fights to recover pieces of MRK1's memory. Every run, you dig up a little more of its past: who built it, and what they built it to do.
  • MORE TO PLAY. Difficulties from Story to Brutal, shareable seeds and endless mode.

Creator Codes

Streaming MRK1? We're interested in working with creators on in-game rewards your viewers can unlock with your own codes, like custom card backs and run boosts.

Get in touch early. Codes and rewards ship inside a game update, so they can't be made on the spot. Email us a few weeks before you plan to play, so we can decide on them together and have your codes live by stream day.

  1. We collaborate with you on designing in-game rewards, such as a custom card back, and you approve them before they ship. Your viewers unlock them with codes they redeem in MRK1. Free, and no obligation to post or say anything.
  2. You let us use your channel name and logo in those rewards and to promote MRK1. You keep your branding; we keep the game and the designs we make.
  3. Either of us can end this anytime, for any reason, by email. We deactivate your codes in the next update and stop using your branding in new promotions. Rewards stay in the game for players who already unlocked them, including any design with your branding, and content already published can stay up.
