About

MRK1 is a roguelite narrative puzzle thriller about an abandoned game simulator that finally has someone to play with. It starts as a simple game on a grid, but the rules keep changing. Draft pieces that finish two lines at once, stack multipliers into huge combos, slot ROM chips that rewrite how you score, and pick your route to each boss.

MRK1 is a charming host, a sore loser and very glad you came. Each run it pushes the game it was taught a little further, and questions its own rules a little harder. The longer you play, the less sure you are what you're helping it become.

For fans of: the score-chasing of Balatro, the creeping unease of Inscryption, the hard calls of Papers, Please.

Highlights

  • The opponent talks back. MRK1 comments on your moves, shops from the same shelf and builds its own run to beat yours.
  • Tic-tac-toe, bent out of shape. Scoring, multipliers and ROM chips stretch the board you know, and every boss rewrites it.
  • It wants your judgment. MRK1 asks hard questions with no clean answer, and remembers what you told it.
  • Funny first, unsettling later. Good company early on. Over the runs, you start wondering what it was built for.
