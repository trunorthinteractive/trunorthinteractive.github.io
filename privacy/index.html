Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy covers MRK1 and this website, both made by TruNorth Interactive, Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. TruNorth Interactive does not run any servers of its own, does not sell personal information, and does not use it for advertising.

What the game uses

  • Save files and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Your name and rough location. To address you in dialogue, the game may read your first name from your computer's user account or Steam profile, and guess your nearest city from your device's region and time zone settings. This stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. In streamer mode the game avoids showing your real name and city on screen.
  • Steam. If you play through Steam, the game sends Valve your achievements, gameplay statistics (such as runs played and where they ended), leaderboard scores and a short status of what you are doing. Valve handles this under the Steam Privacy Policy. Gameplay statistics can be turned off with the analytics option in the settings. We only see statistics combined across all players, plus leaderboard entries that Steam shows publicly.
  • Discord. If the Discord app is running on your computer, the game sends it a short status (such as "Shopping" or the current opponent and how long the run has lasted), which Discord shows on your profile. The game does not sign you in to Discord or read anything from your Discord account. Discord handles this under the Discord Privacy Policy, and you can hide it with Discord's activity privacy settings.

What the website uses

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts, so those services receive your IP address when you visit, under the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy. If you email us, we keep your email only to reply.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU or UK GDPR, or California's CCPA), you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or complain to your local privacy authority. Since we hold almost none, most requests concern Steam or Discord and are best made to them directly. For anything we hold, email trunorthinteractive@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
